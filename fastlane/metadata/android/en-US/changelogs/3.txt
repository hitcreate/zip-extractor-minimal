Adds CPIO, AR/.deb, LZMA, Z and Brotli readers, and tar.z.

Extraction is now single-pass and streaming across all formats, so large archives
no longer need to be buffered in memory, and cancellation takes effect between
entries.

Also fixes non-UTF-8 (CP437) filename decoding for legacy ZIPs whose names were
written before the UTF-8 flag existed.
