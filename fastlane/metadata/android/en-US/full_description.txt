A tiny archive extractor that does one job and stays out of your way.

No ads. No analytics. No trackers. No accounts. Nothing is ever uploaded - extraction happens entirely on your device, and the app requests no permissions at all.

WHAT IT READS

The format is detected from the file's magic bytes rather than its extension, so a mislabelled .zip that is really a 7z still opens.

  ZIP            plain, ZipCrypto, WinZip AES, split volumes
  7z
  RAR / RAR5     including password-protected
  TAR            plain, .tar.gz, .tar.bz2, .tar.xz, .tar.z
  Single-file    .gz, .bz2, .xz, .lzma, .z, .br
  CPIO
  AR / .deb

A password field covers encrypted ZIP, 7z and RAR.

NOT SUPPORTED

Multi-volume sets (.part1.rar, .7z.001), zstd (.zst / .tar.zst), and PPMd-compressed 7z.

WHY NO PERMISSIONS

The app reads files through the system file picker, so it never needs access to your
storage. It is granted a single file, for a single extraction, and only when you
choose it. There is nothing to revoke afterwards.

EXTRACTION SAFETY

Entry paths are checked before anything is written, so a crafted archive cannot
write outside the folder you picked. This is a zip-slip guard, and it is on for
every format, not just ZIP.

SIZE

Around 1.8 MB. Built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.
